Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. This page lists the cookies on xrayinspector.dev and on xRay's sign-in pages. How we process personal data in general is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Strictly necessary cookies

These are always on, because the site or the sign-in does not work without them. They need no consent.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cc_cookie` | xrayinspector.dev | Remembers your cookie choice | 6 months |
| `xray_sso_flow` | xRay sign-in | Links the sign-in page to your sign-in attempt | 10 minutes, or when the sign-in finishes |
| `xray_session` | xRay sign-in | Keeps you signed in to the admin portal | 30 days, or when you sign out |

## Analytics cookies, only with your consent

If you accept analytics cookies, the website loads Google Analytics 4, which sets these cookies. Without your consent, nothing from Google is loaded and these cookies are never set.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visits from the same browser apart | 13 months |
| `_ga_589QLTH004` | Google Analytics | Keeps the state of your visit | 13 months |

Google receives your IP address and device information and may process them in the USA. Google keeps the analytics data for <mark class="legal-placeholder">[[GA data retention period]]</mark>.

## Changing your choice

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies; declining is as easy as accepting. You can change your choice at any time:

<p><button type="button" class="legal-button" data-cc="show-preferencesModal">Open cookie settings</button></p>

The same **Cookie settings** link is at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw consent, the analytics cookies are deleted. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser's settings.

The documentation on docs.page uses no analytics cookies.
