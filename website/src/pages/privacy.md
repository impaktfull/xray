## Who we are

Questions and requests about your data: [privacy@impaktfull.com](mailto:privacy@impaktfull.com).

This page covers xRay only. You sign in to xRay with your impaktfull account, which is described in the [impaktfull account privacy policy](https://account.impaktfull.com/privacy). For impaktfull's website and other services, see the [impaktfull privacy policy](https://www.impaktfull.com/privacy-policy).

## What xRay does not collect

The xRay SDK runs inside your own Flutter app. What it captures (network calls, logs, stored values, files) goes only to the xRay desktop apps on your network that connect to your app. That data never reaches impaktfull or any other server.

If that data contains personal data of your app's users, your company is the controller of it and decides how it is used. Use xRay only in development and internal test builds, with test accounts where you can. The [Quick Start](https://docs.page/impaktfull/xray/getting-started) explains how.

## The website (xrayinspector.dev)

- **Hosting.** The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Fonts and scripts.** The fonts, the cookie banner and the site's scripts are served from xrayinspector.dev itself. Nothing is loaded from a third party unless you accept analytics.
- **Analytics, only with your consent.** If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) to count visits and see which parts of the site are used: pages viewed, clicks on links and buttons, how far you scroll. Google receives your IP address and device information and may process them in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, and we never send your email address or other details you type. Google keeps this data for <mark class="legal-placeholder">[[GA data retention period]]</mark>. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page; the analytics cookies are then deleted. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for the cookies themselves.
- **Access requests.** When you request access to xRay, we store your email address, the date and our decision, to review your request and email you the outcome. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before using xRay (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If we decline, we keep the request for 12 months so we recognise a repeat request; if we grant access, for as long as you have access.

## Your xRay account (desktop app and admin portal)

You sign in with your impaktfull account. impaktfull's sign-in service passes xRay your email address, your name and an account identifier. Your impaktfull account itself (sign-in codes, sessions, security logs, connected Google or Microsoft accounts) is described in the [impaktfull account privacy policy](https://account.impaktfull.com/privacy).

For xRay, we store:

- your email address, name and account identifier;
- when you signed in, and from which installations: the device name set on your computer (often its host name), platform, app version and, for the admin portal, your browser's user agent;
- whether you have access, your access requests and our decisions;
- the service emails we sent you;
- for admins, the changes they make, in an audit log.

Legal basis: providing xRay to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long we keep it:

- sign-in sessions: 90 days after they end;
- installations you no longer use: 12 months after their last use;
- sent emails: 90 days (180 days for emails that could not be delivered);
- declined access requests: 12 months;
- the audit log: 24 months;
- your account: until you delete it.

The xRay platform runs on servers of <mark class="legal-placeholder">Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany [[confirm]]</mark>.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your xRay account in the desktop app under **Settings → Account → Delete account**, or ask us at [privacy@impaktfull.com](mailto:privacy@impaktfull.com). This deletes your xRay account, installations, sessions and access, and removes your address from our records.

Deleting your xRay account does not delete your impaktfull account, which you may use for other impaktfull products. You delete that one under **Profile → GDPR** on [account.impaktfull.com](https://account.impaktfull.com).

## The desktop app

The desktop app does not send analytics or crash reports. It contacts:

- our server (api.xrayinspector.dev) to sign you in and to check for updates;
- impaktfull's sign-in service (auth.impaktfull.com) when you sign in;
- GitHub (api.github.com and github.com) to show the roadmap and to download updates, which shows your IP address to GitHub.

What you inspect, export and configure in the app stays on your computer. **Settings → Account → Clear local data** removes the history the app keeps on your computer.
